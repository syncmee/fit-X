Privacy Policy

Your data is your property. This document explains exactly what we collect, how we process it to train your AI coach, and the controls you have over your information. No fine print, no selling data.

01 Introduction

fiT-X ("we", "us", "our") operates the fiT-X application and related services (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use our Service.

By creating an account and using fiT-X, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. We process your data solely to provide AI-driven coaching, recalibrate your fitness targets, and display your progress.

02 Data We Collect

Biometric & Health Data

To calculate your metabolic rate and daily targets, we collect your sex, age, height, current weight, and daily weigh-ins. If integrated, we also collect sleep, heart rate (RHR/HRV), and step counts from Apple Health, Google Fit, or Oura.

Nutrition & Training Data

We store the meals you log (including macros and calories), your daily hydration levels, and your workout logs (sets, reps, weight, RPE, and exercise variations).

Account & Usage Data

Your name, email address, and app usage metadata (such as login times, feature interactions, and crash reports) to maintain your account and improve the Service.

03 How We Use Your Data

Your data is the fuel for the fiT-X AI Coach. We use it specifically to:

  • ›Calculate your TDEE, BMR, and daily calorie/macro targets using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation.
  • ›Recalibrate your training volume, weekly schedule, and targets daily at 04:30 based on recovery metrics.
  • ›Render your progress charts, adherence heatmaps, and strength curves in the dashboard.
  • ›Send push notifications for daily check-ins, scheduled workouts, and coach adjustments.

04 Sharing & Integrations

We do not sell your data. We do not share your information with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

We only share data with third-party service providers who help us operate the app:

  • ›Cloud Infrastructure: AWS / Vercel for secure hosting and database management.
  • ›Wearable Integrations: Apple Health, Google Fit, and Oura API—only when you explicitly authorize the connection.
  • ›Analytics: Anonymous, aggregated usage data to monitor app stability.

05 Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal data. All data in transit is encrypted using TLS 1.3, and data at rest is encrypted using AES-256.

Access to production databases is strictly limited to authorized engineering personnel and is protected by hardware security keys (FIDO2) and IP whitelisting. Despite our efforts, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

06 Your Rights

You are in full control of your data. Depending on your location (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you have the following rights:

Access & Export

You can request a copy of all your data in CSV or JSON format via the Settings menu.

Rectification

You can update your biometrics, email, and goals at any time in the app.

Erasure (Right to be Forgotten)

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data from the Account tab in Settings.

Opt-Out

You can disable push notifications and disconnect wearables at any time.
